# Provider icons

`Assets.xcassets/Provider*.imageset` hold the Claude, OpenAI and Grok marks, taken from
[`@lobehub/icons-static-svg`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@lobehub/icons-static-svg) 1.103.0 (MIT licence),
reduced to a single black path so they work as template images.

The marks are trademarks of Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI respectively. They are used only to identify
which provider a subscription belongs to.
